# BetBuddy — Investor Pitch Deck (10 Slides)

## Slide 1 — Vision
**BetBuddy is the social layer for sports betting.**

- Bettors already place bets; BetBuddy makes performance visible, competitive, and shareable.
- We turn isolated betting into a networked game of skill and reputation.

**Core thesis:** Betting has liquidity and attention, but lacks a trusted social graph.

---

## Slide 2 — Problem
### Betting experience is broken across three silos:
1. **Bookmakers:** Where bets are placed.
2. **Social apps:** Where discussion and bragging happen.
3. **Spreadsheets/screenshots:** Where “proof” of results lives.

### Why this matters:
- No trusted, unified betting identity.
- No verified long-term performance record.
- Low post-bet engagement and weak retention.

---

## Slide 3 — Solution
### BetBuddy = **Tracking + Social + Competition**

- **Verified bet tracking** (manual now, API-enabled later).
- **Public performance profiles:** ROI, win rate, streaks, sport splits.
- **Head-to-head (H2H) challenges:** users oppose/accept picks with transparent outcomes.
- **Social feed and leagues:** follows, reactions, private groups, rankings.

**Outcome:** Better trust, stronger engagement, and habit-forming product loops.

---

## Slide 4 — Why Now
- Sports betting participation is mainstream and mobile-first.
- Creator/tipster economy is growing, but trust infrastructure is weak.
- Social products win on retention; betting products win on transaction intent.

**BetBuddy captures both:** entertainment engagement + performance accountability.

---

## Slide 5 — Product Roadmap
### Phase 1 (0–3 months): Foundation
- Accounts, tracked bets, performance profiles.

### Phase 2 (3–6 months): H2H Engine
- Challenge creation, acceptance, settlement logic, challenge history.

### Phase 3 (6–9 months): Social Layer
- Feed, following, comments/reactions, share cards.

### Phase 4 (9–12 months): Gamification + Monetisation
- Leaderboards, private leagues, premium analytics, affiliate placements.

---

## Slide 6 — Business Model
### 1) Freemium subscription (primary)
- **Free:** core tracking + limited H2H.
- **Premium (£5–£15/month):** advanced analytics, unlimited H2H, elite competitions, profile boosts.

### 2) Affiliate revenue (high-margin)
- CPA + rev-share with bookmakers.

### 3) Social monetisation
- Paid leagues, featured placements, verified tipster subscriptions.

### 4) Ads (long-term only)
- Introduced after retention and engagement stabilize.

---

## Slide 7 — Go-To-Market
### Beachhead audience:
- University networks, betting group chats, local football communities.

### Distribution mechanics:
- Viral H2H result cards.
- Public leaderboards and “call-out” dynamics.
- Tipster onboarding with verified profile migration.
- Weekly content loops: top bettors, fades, biggest swings.

---

## Slide 8 — Competitive Positioning
| Segment | Current Options | Structural Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Bookmakers | Bet365, Betfair, etc. | No social identity/reputation layer |
| Tipster channels | X, Telegram, WhatsApp | No verification or standardized performance |
| Tracking tools | Sheets/basic apps | No native competition/community loop |

**BetBuddy moat:** verified identity + social graph + challenge mechanics + historical performance data.

---

## Slide 9 — Metrics, Regulation, and Risk Control
### North-star metrics:
- DAU/WAU, bets per active user, H2H per active user.
- Week-4 and week-12 retention.
- Premium conversion and affiliate revenue per active user.

### Regulatory strategy:
- **Phase 1:** points-based competition (no peer-to-peer real money).
- **Phase 2 (optional):** licensed real-money formats once compliance economics are justified.

### Key risks and mitigations:
- Fraud/false records → verification tooling and audit trails.
- Churn after novelty → recurring leagues, streak rewards, social loops.
- Compliance complexity → staged market entry and legal-first roadmap.
